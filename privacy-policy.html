Application: Pop or Stop · Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QuatroIT ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the mobile game Pop or Stop (the "App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described below. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Who we are

The App is developed and published by QuatroIT. For any questions about this Policy or about your data, contact us at: quatro.fan.game@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email, or any other personal details to play. The App does not access your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, or location, and it does not connect to our servers.

The current version of the App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no in-app purchases.

3. Data stored on your device

To remember your best score and your settings (sound, music, and vibration), the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

4. Device permissions

  • Vibration. The App may use the device's vibration motor for haptic feedback during play. Vibration can be turned on or off at any time in the App's settings.

5. Third-party services

The App is built with the Unity engine and is distributed through Google Play. Google Play may process data related to downloading and installing apps under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The App itself does not send data to Unity or to any other third party.

If we add advertising or analytics in a future version, we will update this Policy before that version is released and describe exactly which data is processed and by whom.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect personal data, we do not keep any data about you on our side. All data the App uses is stored only on your device; you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

8. Your rights
